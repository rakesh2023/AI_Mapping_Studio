About These Settings

Confidence thresholds control how the AI Mapping Generator and Validation engine classify mappings across the app:

HighConfidence >= High threshold. Safe to auto-approve. MediumConfidence between Medium and High. Reviewed but usually correct. LowConfidence below Medium. Always requires human review.
Changes apply immediately across the AI Mapping Generator, Mapping Workspace, and Validation pages - no page reload required for other open tabs on next navigation.
